Privacy Policy

Gym Tracker · effective 31 July 2026

In short: Gym Tracker stores your workout data only on your own device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. If you connect Apple Health, that data is read with your permission, stays on your device, and is never uploaded anywhere.

Who this is from

Gym Tracker is built and maintained by an individual developer, Dan Cross, not a company. Any question about this policy or the app can be sent to the contact address at the bottom of this page.

What data the app holds

Gym Tracker records the workouts, exercises and sets you log yourself: exercise names, weights, reps, sets, dates, notes and any routines you create. All of this is stored locally on your device — in the app's local storage on iPhone, or in your browser's storage if you use the web version. Nothing is sent to a server, because there is no server. Nobody but you can see this data.

Apple Health

If you use the iPhone app and choose to connect Apple Health, the app asks iOS for permission to read specific types of health data: workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, body weight, sleep, VO₂max and active energy. This only happens if you tap "Connect Apple Health" — nothing is read beforehand.

  • Health data read from Apple Health is stored only on your device, inside the app's own local storage, so it can be shown to you in the app's Statistics screen.
  • It is never uploaded to a server, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising or analytics.
  • If you turn on "Save workouts to Apple Health" in Settings, the app also writes your finished gym workouts back into Apple Health, so they show up alongside your other activity there. This only happens with that setting switched on.
  • You can disconnect Apple Health at any time from Settings → Apple Health → Disconnect. You can additionally choose "Disconnect and remove imported data" to delete the Apple Health data stored in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data, including anything read from Apple Health, from your device.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising, and no data is sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No cloud sync or backend server at all — everything described above is on-device only.

Data you import

If you import workout history from a CSV file (for example, an export from another tracking app), that file is read and processed entirely on your device and stored the same way as data you log directly. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere.

Children

Gym Tracker is a general-purpose fitness tracker, not directed at children, and collects no personal data from anyone since nothing leaves the device in the first place.
