Privacy Policy

Gym Tracker · effective 3 September 2026

In short: Gym Tracker stores your workout data only on your own device. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising. If you connect Apple Health, that data is read with your permission and stays on your device. The optional AI Coach, which you switch on by entering your own Anthropic API key, sends a summary of your gym data to Anthropic under your own account — and never your Apple Health data unless you separately turn that on.

Who this is from

Gym Tracker is built and maintained by an individual developer, Dan Cross, not a company. Any question about this policy or the app can be sent to the contact address at the bottom of this page.

What data the app holds

Gym Tracker records the workouts, exercises and sets you log yourself: exercise names, weights, reps, sets, dates, notes and any routines you create. All of this is stored locally on your device — in the app's local storage on iPhone, or in your browser's storage if you use the web version. Nothing is sent to a server, because there is no server. Nobody but you can see this data.

Apple Health

If you use the iPhone app and choose to connect Apple Health, the app asks iOS for permission to read specific types of health data: workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, body weight, sleep, VO₂max and active energy. This only happens if you tap "Connect Apple Health" — nothing is read beforehand.

  • Health data read from Apple Health is stored only on your device, inside the app's own local storage, so it can be shown to you in the app's Statistics screen.
  • It is never uploaded to a server, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising or analytics.
  • If you turn on "Save workouts to Apple Health" in Settings, the app also writes your finished gym workouts back into Apple Health, so they show up alongside your other activity there. This only happens with that setting switched on.
  • You can disconnect Apple Health at any time from Settings → Apple Health → Disconnect. You can additionally choose "Disconnect and remove imported data" to delete the Apple Health data stored in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data, including anything read from Apple Health, from your device.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising, and no data is sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No cloud sync and no backend server of our own — we operate no servers and receive none of your data. The only network use is the optional AI Coach described below, which talks directly to Anthropic using a key you supply.

AI Coach (optional)

The Coach is off until you enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings. It writes a short daily summary of your training, reviews each session you finish, and keeps a return-to-training plan up to date. To do that, the app sends a request from your device directly to Anthropic's Claude API (api.anthropic.com), authenticated with your key and billed to your own Anthropic account.

  • What is sent: a derived summary of your gym data — weekly set counts per muscle group, per-exercise progress figures (weights, reps, estimated one-rep max), the sets from the session just finished, and the short profile you type in (goal, training days, any injury or equipment notes). Not your full history, and nothing about who you are.
  • Apple Health is excluded by default. Only if you also switch on "Share recovery data with coach" in Settings will four derived values be added: last night's sleep duration, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and your body-weight trend. Raw Health samples are never sent under any setting.
  • Your key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Anthropic, as the authentication header of those requests. We never see it.
  • Anthropic processes these requests under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We receive nothing — the reply comes straight back to your device and is stored there.
  • Removing the key in Settings turns the Coach off entirely; "Clear coach data" deletes every summary and plan it produced.

Data you import

If you import workout history from a CSV file (for example, an export from another tracking app), that file is read and processed entirely on your device and stored the same way as data you log directly. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere.

Children

Gym Tracker is a general-purpose fitness tracker, not directed at children, and collects no personal data from anyone — the developer operates no service that could receive any.
